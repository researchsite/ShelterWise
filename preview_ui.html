Governing San Francisco & California Statutes

Retrieved statutes providing affirmative defenses against unlawful detainer

San Francisco Rent Ordinance Just Cause & Universal Right to Counsel SF Admin. Code Ch. 37 & Prop F

Requires enumerated just cause for residential eviction, caps annual rent adjustments on covered units, and guarantees city-funded legal representation within 30 days of unlawful detainer service.

California Implied Warranty of Habitability & Rent Demand Bar Cal. Civ. Code § 1941.1 & § 1942.4

Bars landlords from demanding rent, increasing rent, or serving a 3-day pay-or-quit notice when an official building/housing citation for untenantable residential conditions (missing heat, hot water, weatherproofing, plumbing, or safe wiring) remains unabated past 35 days.

California Statutory Anti-Retaliation Protection Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5

Establishes a 180-day statutory bar against retaliatory eviction, rent hikes, or service reductions after a residential tenant complains to a municipal building inspection agency.
