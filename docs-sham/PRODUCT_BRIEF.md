# Product brief

## Name

**ShelterWise**

## One-line description

An AI-assisted housing stability navigator that helps tenants and advocates turn housing distress into an evidence-backed plan for human review.

## Problem

A tenant facing unsafe housing, an eviction notice, or financial hardship often has to navigate disconnected systems: municipal complaint records, housing rules, court processes, rental assistance, and local service providers. Information is fragmented, legal language is difficult to interpret, and a tenant may not know what evidence is missing or what to do next.

## Primary users

- **Tenant:** explains the situation and receives understandable next steps.
- **Housing advocate / paralegal:** reviews evidence, checks sources, and prepares a case summary.
- **Attorney / qualified professional:** uses the brief as an intake aid, not as a substitute for legal analysis.

## MVP outcome

Generate a structured Tenant Stabilization Brief that includes:
- tenant-reported facts, clearly labeled as reported;
- relevant public-record evidence, with source and timestamp;
- relevant legal or program information, with jurisdiction and citations;
- missing evidence and uncertainty;
- practical next steps and referral options;
- explicit human-review flags.

## Product differentiators

1. **Evidence before answer:** every factual claim is traceable to a source or clearly marked as tenant-reported.
2. **Explain why:** show why a record matched and why nearby records were excluded.
3. **Missing-evidence workflow:** absence of a 311 record is not evidence that no problem exists.
4. **Human-centered output:** a concise case workspace and brief, not a generic chatbot.
5. **Equity-aware analysis:** use aggregate neighborhood context to identify data gaps, never to make individual eligibility or risk decisions based on protected traits.

## Non-goals for the hackathon MVP

- Filing a complaint or court document automatically.
- Giving definitive legal advice or guaranteeing eligibility.
- Predicting whether a tenant will be evicted.
- Contacting landlords, courts, or government agencies on a tenant's behalf.
- Making decisions using race, ethnicity, disability, immigration status, or other sensitive traits.
- Building a full case-management platform.

## Success measures

- A user can reach a useful draft brief in a few guided steps.
- Each public-record or legal-resource claim has a source.
- Irrelevant/decoy records are excluded with an understandable reason.
- The UI distinguishes confirmed facts, user statements, contextual evidence, and unknowns.
- The demo clearly communicates one practical path toward stability.
