# Demo script

Target length: 3–4 minutes.

## Scenario

A tenant reports that the apartment has had no heat, there is water near an electrical outlet, and they received a notice to pay or leave. They are unsure what evidence to gather or where to seek help. Treat all scenario facts as illustrative unless a real, consented test case is used.

## Walkthrough

### 1. Start with the problem (20–30 sec)
“People dealing with unsafe housing or eviction pressure often have to search across disconnected city, legal, and assistance systems. ShelterWise turns that fragmented intake into a reviewable path forward.”

### 2. Enter the case (30 sec)
Enter the narrative and selected metro. Show extracted facts and allow the user to correct them.

### 3. Show evidence intelligence (45 sec)
Display relevant public records with source, date, status, geography, and match rationale. Show at least one excluded decoy and explain the legitimate exclusion reason. Explicitly say: “This record provides public context; it does not prove the condition inside this tenant’s unit.”

### 4. Show legal/resources (40 sec)
Display official sources and plain-language summaries. Emphasize that eligibility and deadlines require human verification.

### 5. Show missing evidence (30 sec)
If no matching 311 record exists, explain that absence does not disprove the tenant’s account. Suggest safe next steps such as preserving notices, dated photos, or contacting the appropriate inspection/advocacy resource.

### 6. Generate the brief (45 sec)
Show the structured brief with clear labels, citations, uncertainty, and reviewer checklist.

### 7. Close with impact (15 sec)
“ShelterWise does not replace an advocate or attorney. It helps them start with a clearer, better-sourced picture and a practical next step.”

## Demo discipline

- Use a preloaded sample case so the demo does not depend on typing speed.
- Keep one live interaction to prove the system works.
- Never claim a record is from the tenant’s exact unit unless verified.
- Never claim the tool determines legal eligibility or prevents eviction.
