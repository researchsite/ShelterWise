# Tenant Stabilization Brief — output schema

The generated brief is a draft for advocate/paralegal/attorney review.

## 1. Case snapshot

- Case reference (non-identifying):
- Metro / jurisdiction:
- Date prepared:
- Main concern:
- Time-sensitive dates (tenant-reported; verify):
- Immediate safety flag:

## 2. Tenant-reported situation

Summarize the tenant's own account in plain language. Preserve uncertainty and dates. Do not state reported allegations as independently verified facts.

## 3. Evidence found

For each item:
- Evidence label:
- Source / publisher:
- Original record ID:
- Source date / last updated:
- Location precision:
- What matched:
- Why relevant:
- What this evidence does not establish:
- Source link:

## 4. Legal information to review

For each item:
- Jurisdiction:
- Topic:
- Plain-language summary:
- Official source / citation:
- Effective or last-checked date:
- Applicability conditions:
- Questions for qualified reviewer:

## 5. Potential assistance and referrals

For each program/resource:
- Program name:
- Official provider:
- Eligibility conditions to confirm:
- Required documents:
- Application/contact route:
- Current availability status:
- Source link:
- Human verification needed:

## 6. Evidence gaps

| Missing item | Why it may matter | Safe next step |
|---|---|---|
| Example: dated photos | May help document condition | Preserve original files and dates |
| Example: written notice | Needed to understand deadlines | Keep the complete notice and envelope |

Do not recommend unsafe evidence collection or confrontation.

## 7. Suggested next steps

Order by urgency. Distinguish emergency steps from administrative steps. Avoid guarantees.

## 8. Uncertainty and limitations

State stale data, missing fields, geographic imprecision, ambiguous legal applicability, and unavailable program status.

## 9. Reviewer checklist

- [ ] Tenant-reported facts confirmed with tenant.
- [ ] Public records refer to the correct geography/property context.
- [ ] Current law and deadlines checked.
- [ ] Program eligibility and availability verified.
- [ ] Safety concerns appropriately escalated.
- [ ] Brief approved by qualified human before external use.
