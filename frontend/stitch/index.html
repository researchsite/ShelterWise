Architecture Overview: Google Stitch for Public-Interest Tech

The Google Stitch Design System in ShelterWise establishes a component-driven, trauma-informed architecture. When low-income tenants facing eviction arrive at this platform, they are often in acute crisis. Stitch replaces cold, dense technical tables with calm, reassuring, highly legible components with clear calls to action and instant distress overrides.

5 Tiers: Tokens → Atoms → Molecules → Organisms → Templates
WCAG AAA: Verified color contrast > 7:1 for critical legal notifications
Trauma-Informed: Distress mode overrides for cognitive overload
