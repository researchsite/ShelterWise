Engineering Philosophy · Google ADK Architecture

"Scope beats ambition, and a real job is deciding what NOT to build."

We did not build an ungrounded chatbot to replace attorneys. We engineered an autonomous evidentiary bridge linking tenant lived narratives to authentic City Hall public records.

❌ What We Decided NOT to Build
✕
No Ungrounded Chatbot Hallucinations Generic LLMs invent fake court citations, giving vulnerable families false confidence that results in default eviction.
✕
No Unauthorized Practice of Law ShelterWise never gives unguided legal advice. It prepares certified evidentiary packets for paralegal and attorney review.
✕
No Demographic Case Scoring Bias Demographic attributes (race, language, vehicle access) are strictly isolated behind a Responsible AI firewall during case triage.
✕
No Commercial or Closed Lookalikes Actively filters out warehouse decoys, resolved 2024 complaints, and zero-violation inspections that would harm tenant credibility.

Decoy Rejection Engine with Statutory Business Rules Rejects commercial properties under SF Housing Code § 701 and abated repairs to ensure unassailable court evidence.
