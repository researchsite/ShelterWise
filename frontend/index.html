    The 8 Grounded Rules (All Tested in Code)

    1. Search wide, then filter: Vector search retrieves candidate semantic hits; Python rules filter out decoys.
    2. Strict Residential Keep Rule: Keep only if property_type == 'Residential' and closed_date IS NULL.
    3. Zero Decoy Leaks: Never hardcode "DECOY". Filter strictly on business rules (Commercial, Abated, No Violation).
    4. Relief Funds Verification: Grants unlock only when prerequisites (such as open DBI records) are satisfied.
    5. Statutory Repair Clocks: Calculates 35-day repair window under CA Civil Code § 1942.4 from citation date.
    6. Eviction Court Grain: Pre-aggregates 4 quarters of court filings per tract to prevent 400% over-counting.
    7. 11-Digit Text GEOIDs: Preserves leading zeros (06075...) in census tract identifiers.
    8. Demographic Firewall: audit_* columns are strictly isolated from individual case triage.

    Ethical AI Guardrails & Human-in-the-Loop

    • Grounding Before Answer: Every external claim cites BigQuery provenance or is labeled [Tenant-Reported].
    • Absence ≠ Proof: The absence of a 311 record never implies conditions are safe.
    • Block ≠ Unit: Building-level 311 records corroborate neglect but do not replace unit-specific testimony.
    • Mandatory Attorney Review: A 7-item checklist must be signed off by counsel before filing court motions.
    • Strict Data Privacy: Tenant intake sandboxed; protected demographic traits are never used to score tenants.
