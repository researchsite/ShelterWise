The 8 Grounded Rules of AI-Assisted Eviction Defense

    

      

        
Rule 1: Always Corroborate via Municipal Records

        
A tenant assertion alone is easily disputed by landlords. Pairing it with a certified City 311 citation provides irrefutable public corroboration.

      

      

        
Rule 2: Disqualify Commercial & Abated Decoys

        
Filtering nearby false leads prevents citing irrelevant commercial storefronts or pre-repaired violations in court.

      

      

        
Rule 3: Enforce the 35-Day Clock Under § 1942.4

        
If 35 days have passed since notice of violation without complete repair, the landlord is legally prohibited from demanding rent.

      

      

        
Rule 4: Invoke the 180-Day Retaliation Shield

        
Under Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5, any eviction notice served within 180 days of a good-faith habitability complaint is presumed retaliatory.

      

      

        
Rule 5: Coarsen Addresses to 100-Block Range

        
To protect tenant safety and privacy, public queries are coarsened to the 100-block level rather than precise apartment numbers.

      

      

        
Rule 6: Isolate Demographic Data Behind a Firewall

        
Individual demographic attributes must never influence legal merit or grant prioritization.

      

      

        
Rule 7: Mandatory Human Attorney Review

        
No legal pleading is submitted to San Francisco Superior Court without explicit attorney sign-off via transactional lock.

      

      

        
Rule 8: Connect Tenants to Prop F Right to Counsel

        
Every San Francisco renter has a universal right to free legal representation at 400 McAllister St.
